# Flow of Program Assignments

1. Input a year and determine whether it is a leap year or not.

2. Take two numbers as input and print the sum of both numbers.

3. Take a number as input and print its multiplication table.

4. Take two numbers as input and find their HCF (Highest Common Factor) and LCM (Least Common Multiple).

5. Keep taking numbers as input until the user enters `x`. After that, print the sum of all the entered numbers.
